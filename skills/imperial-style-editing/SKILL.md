# Imperial Style Editing (`imperial-style-editing`)

A production framework for coding agents and editors to craft **grand, immersive historical and philosophical documentaries** in the style of **Imperial (@IMPERIALYT)**. Characterized by expansive 3D camera movements across historical artifacts, dark volumetric environments, philosophical pacing, and rich cinematic soundscapes.

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic DNA & Narrative Stance

- **Tone & Persona:** Grand, solemn, philosophical, investigative, timeless.
- **Visual Stance:** Treat history not as a textbook, but as an ancient, shadowy gallery through which the camera travels. The viewer is a solitary explorer walking through the corridors of time.
- **Key Visual Elements:** Classical statues, antique paintings, crumbling monuments, flickering candlelight, volumetric fog, and slow, deliberate 3D camera pans.

---

## 2. Timeline Architecture & Pacing Rules

- **Pacing Rhythm:** Deliberate slow-burn with sudden philosophical epiphanies.
- **Shot Duration:** 5.0 to 8.0 seconds per scene. Never rush; allow the 3D depth and atmospheric particles to establish emotional weight.
- **Fade Dynamics:** Long cross-dissolves (24–36 frames) or fades through pitch black to transition between historical eras.

---

## 3. 3D Camera & Volumetric Environment (After Effects / Blender)

- **Atmospheric 3D Space:**
  - Historical paintings and character portraits positioned as 2.5D planes in 3D space.
  - Volumetric light rays (God Rays) piercing through darkness using CC Light Rays or Blender volumetric scatter.
  - Floating dust motes and embers drifting gently through the light beam.
- **Camera Movement:**
  - Continuous slow drift: Camera drifts along both Z (depth) and X (horizontal) axes simultaneously.
  - Wide lenses (`28mm` to `35mm`) to emphasize grandeur and scale.

---

## 4. Typography & Title Design

- **Primary Font:** Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or Cinzel Decorative.
- **Styling:**
  - Classic serif, centered, elegant tracking (`+250`).
  - Subtle opacity fade with slow forward scale creep (100% to 104% over 8 seconds).
  - Quotes rendered in muted off-white or aged parchment cream (`#E2DAC8`).

---

## 6. Sound Design & Audio Architecture

- **Voiceover:** Deep, resonant, gravelly or cinematic baritone voiceover. Mastered with rich low-mids (`200Hz`) and subtle studio plate reverb (`Decay: 1.2s`, `Wet: 8%`).
- **Soundscape:**
  - Low cello drones sustaining beneath philosophical pauses.
  - Deep taiko / orchestral bass drum hits on major historical dates.
  - Environmental winds, distant thunder, echoing footsteps in stone cathedrals.
- **Music:** Neo-classical orchestral, ambient drone soundscapes, Gregorian chants, or solemn piano melodies.\n
