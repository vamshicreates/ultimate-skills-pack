# Evidence and casting decisions

Use this reference to resolve doubtful cast, identity, recent-film, age, or role claims.

## Cast and identity

- Prefer the source film's IMDb full cast or official end credits as the starting boundary. Cross-check names that appear only in search snippets or secondary listings.
- Keep similarly named actors separate. A person's filmography must match the cast profile, and a credit listed under a producer, musician, or crew profile is not enough to infer acting roles.
- If a prominent person is named by a secondary cast list but absent from the primary cast list, research the discrepancy and report it. Do not add a speculative row just to improve coverage.

## Three-film evidence

- Select up to three released feature films per performer as of the checked date. Use actual release information where the current year's status or order is unclear. Do not include upcoming titles just because they have a year on IMDb.
- A title and a character name are factual credits, not a role category. To infer `father`, `teacher`, `police officer`, `comic supporting role`, etc., seek a credit description, character page, biography, or other reliable attribution that establishes that type.
- A sequel with the same character provides evidence of continuing work, but counts as one kind of part when judging casting range.
- If IMDb gives no character name, leave it blank. Mark voice, uncredited, or child-artist appearances in the note column; they should not be silently treated as ordinary on-screen adult roles.

## Casting categories

- Prefer a few useful, broad categories over a long speculative list. `Main lead` describes proven lead experience; `comic supporting role` needs comedy work; family and profession categories should have a credible past role or clear wider portfolio support.
- The source film's specific character and its genre do not determine the suggested categories.
- `Needs casting review` is a valid result when credits are sparse, identities conflict, or film titles alone do not establish a role type. Do not infer family relationships from a character name, age, or appearance.

## Age and Instagram

- Compute age from a corroborated date of birth. If only birth year is known and the birthday has not been verified, report an age range in research notes and leave the single age cell blank unless all possible dates yield the same completed age.
- An Instagram profile link must be tied to the exact actor. Search results and guessed handles do not meet that standard.
