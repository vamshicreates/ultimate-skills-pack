# Director's Take

**Director's Take** is a recurring My Logline social video series about the daring creative choice behind a film. Each 30–45 second episode asks what made a director commit to an unlikely idea, shows why that choice works on screen, and invites viewers to write their own film.

## Episode format

1. Open with a question naming the director and film.
2. Explain the film's surprising premise and a verified origin detail when one is available.
3. Identify the director's creative leap and the feeling it creates for the audience.
4. Turn that insight toward the viewer's own wild film idea.
5. Close with the My Logline sign-up and the **mylogline** comment keyword for the DM link.

The tone is curious and appreciative. An episode can discuss a director's documented account of an idea, but it should never invent their private thoughts. It should credit collaborators whose ideas shaped the film.

The [approved *Eega* pilot](references/approved-eega-script.md) sets the structure and cadence. Future episodes should find a film-specific creative choice rather than reuse its phrasing.

## Use the skill

After installing this repository as a Codex skill, name a film and, optionally, its director:

> `$directors-take` S. S. Rajamouli — *Eega*

The skill returns one ready-to-record script and a brief source note for any behind-the-scenes facts. The instructions are in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).
