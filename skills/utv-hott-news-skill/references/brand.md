# UTV Hott News visual and editorial rules

- Canvas: 1080 × 1350 px, four pages per language.
- Brand palette sampled from the user's reference: warm yellow `#EDB73F`, near-black `#111111`, red `#AD2B21`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Typography: use Poppins for every editable English/Latin text layer, including headings, cards, labels, attribution, CTA, and Instagram handle. Poppins has no Telugu glyphs; a Telugu carousel needs a Telugu-capable font for those characters. Never describe fallback Telugu lettering as Poppins. If the user insists on Poppins only, deliver English and ask before making an exception for Telugu. The supplied logo and official store badges remain image assets.
- Logo: the user's `utvnews.png`, bundled as `assets/logo.png`, shown as a small icon at the top left of every page.
- Social mark: use exactly `@utvhottnewsapp` in Poppins Bold (700), 25 px / 38 px line height, about 18.8 pt in Canva. Place a 30 × 30 px Instagram icon immediately before it with an 11 px gap. The footer has 12 px vertical / 16 px horizontal padding, a 12 px corner radius, 54 px right inset and 30 px bottom inset. Vertically center icon and username.
- Footer colors: on black cover/context pages, use yellow `#EDB73F` for the footer background and dark `#141312` for the username, with a dark Instagram icon. On yellow detail/CTA pages, use black `#111111` background, white `#FFFFFF` username and a white icon. The logo is the only small top-left mark; do not add another handle or website.
- Cover: when a suitable source photo is available, use it with a dark gradient at the bottom, yellow headline, and small category pill. The photo should show the subject without cropping faces. For a screenshot without a usable photo, use the text-led yellow and black cover with the same headline treatment; do not place unrelated imagery.
- Page 2: yellow background, black rounded point cards, red number circles, brief factual qualifier when needed.
- Page 3: black background with a yellow top accent clear of the logo, one strong context question, concise explanatory copy. Source metadata is internal by default; public slides do not mention screenshots or “UTV reports.”
- Page 4: topic-specific question and a clear invitation to download UTV Hott News App. Place the cropped `assets/store-badges-approved.png` beneath the download panel, with both App Store and Google Play badges visible. Match the edited reference: black badges with yellow lettering/Apple mark and yellow surrounding space, without a white rectangular mat. Use the exact transparent badge artwork saved from the user-edited Canva. Its transparent lettering and outer area reveal the yellow slide beneath; retain the original Google Play triangle colors. CSS blend modes and filters were ignored by Canva, so use the approved asset rather than trying to tint the old white-mat image. Keep the same logo and Instagram footer. Do not imply a clickable store link unless one has been provided and checked.
- Alignment: page headings and body text are left-aligned on the 54 px grid. The approved two-line cover starts its category at y≈744 px, headline at y≈847 px, and one-line subhead at y≈1050 px. CSS uses a 55 px category-to-headline margin and 38 px headline-to-subhead margin. Keep this rhythm for new content; shorten copy or shift the whole block upward when an extra line would enter the footer area. Do not leave the old large blank gap above the cover headline.
- Fact cards: x=54 px, width=972 px, height=160 px, with 20 px gaps. Text begins 105 px inside each card (page x=159 px), with 28 px right padding. Use Poppins ExtraBold, English size 42 px / 1.16 line height, **left alignment**, and vertical centering. Wrapped lines start at the same x position; number circles remain centered separately. Do not center the card copy.
- Shape layering: keep shapes empty and text in independent layers above them. Center category/context labels, number-circle text and download-panel labels; apply the fact-card left alignment exception. Footer text follows its own left alignment beside the icon. Keep the context tag optically centered (the reference moved its text about 3 px downward).
- Approved reference: [user-edited Chiranjeevi carousel](https://www.canva.com/design/DAHZLbSfhIA/N3-MIbp4O0AmTpjoVNI-UQ/edit), inspected 27 October 2026. Use these saved previews to check the pattern without depending on Canva access: [cover](approved-layout/page-1.png), [fact cards](approved-layout/page-2.png), [context](approved-layout/page-3.png), [CTA and badges](approved-layout/page-4.png). Content changes between stories; alignment, Poppins typography and footer treatment persist.
- Telugu text should be idiomatic and readable; English copy should be written for English readers, rather than rigidly translated.
- For uncertain developments, use language such as “expected to discuss,” “reportedly considering,” or Telugu equivalents. Preserve any qualification in the source article.
