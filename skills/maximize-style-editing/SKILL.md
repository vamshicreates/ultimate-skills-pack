# Maximize Style Editing (`maximize-style-editing`)

A production framework for coding agents and editors to build **high-converting, hyper-retention short-form videos (Reels, Shorts, TikToks)** in the style of **Maximize (@bymaximise)**. Built from the ground up to prevent user scroll-off through aggressive pacing, animated kinetic subtitles, rapid punch zooms, and continuous sound design triggers.

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic DNA & Retention Engineering

- **Format:** 9:16 Vertical Video (`1080x1920`), 60fps or 30fps.
- **Primary Goal:** Maximum Viewer Retention Rate (>85% average percentage viewed).
- **The Hook Rule:** First 1.5 seconds must feature:
  1. A provocative text hook on screen.
  2. Immediate vocal statement without an intro.
  3. A dynamic zoom or sound effect.

---

## 2. Timeline Architecture & Pacing Rules

- **Cut / Event Frequency:** Every 0.8 to 1.4 seconds.
- **Jump Cuts:** Every breath, silence (>0.15s), or filler word is cut out with frame-accurate precision.
- **Scale Punch-Ins:**
  - Beat 1: Center framing (100% scale).
  - Beat 2: Punch-in on key takeaway (118% scale).
  - Beat 3: Close-up punch on climax (135% scale).
  - Beat 4: Reset to 100%.

---

## 3. Kinetic Subtitles & Typography

- **Primary Font:** The Bold Font, Montserrat Extra Bold, Futura Heavy, or Komika Axis.
- **Word Density:** Only 1 to 3 words displayed on screen at any given millisecond.
- **Styling:**
  - Text color: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Active Word Highlight: Neon Yellow (`#FFE600`), Toxic Green (`#00FF66`), or Vibrant Cyan (`#00E5FF`).
  - Stroke: `12px` solid black stroke + heavy drop shadow (`Distance: 8`, `Softness: 12`, `Opacity: 85%`).
- **Emoji & Sticker Sync:**
  - High-res 3D Apple/Fluent emojis pop in with an overshoot bounce right above the emphasized word.

---

## 4. Visual Effects & B-Roll Overlays

- **B-Roll Pop-Ins:** Relevant 1-second B-roll clips slide in from bottom or right, held for no longer than 1.5 seconds before snapping back to presenter.
- **Screen Shake:** 3-frame directional camera shake on heavy impact words ("Stop", "Never", "Broke").
- **Motion Backgrounds:** Moving grid patterns or ambient particle loops behind rotoscoped subjects.

---

## 5. Sound Design & Audio Pacing

- **Sub-Bass Hits:** 808 thud on every punchline.
- **High-Frequency SFX:**
  - High-pitch "pop" or "blop" on every word change.
  - Camera shutter / flash click on photo reveals.
  - Swoosh-risers sweeping into each zoom-in.
- **Music:** High-tempo phonk, Brazilian bass, or upbeat rhythmic boom-bap running continuously under the speech, sidechain-ducked `-12dB`.\n
