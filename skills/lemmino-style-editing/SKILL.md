# LEMMiNO Style Editing (`lemmino-style-editing`)

A production framework for coding agents and editors to build **forensic, minimalist investigative documentaries** in the style of **LEMMiNO**. Masterclass in visual restraint, precision 3D spatial reconstructions, forensic document examination, and haunting ambient sound design.

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic DNA & Investigative Stance

- **Tone & Persona:** Calm, objective, forensic, mysterious, intellectually patient.
- **Visual Stance:** *"Let the evidence speak."* No flashy cartoon stickers, no exaggerated emotional reactions. Information is presented like an declassified intelligence briefing or forensic courtroom exhibit.
- **Visual Restraint:** The screen is dark, quiet, and precise. Visual elements linger long enough for the audience to study timestamps, flight paths, and architectural floorplans.

---

## 2. 3D Wireframe & Spatial Reconstructions (Blender / After Effects)

- **Orthographic & Isometric Views:**
  - Architectural blueprints and incident locations modeled in clean 3D wireframe or matte clay renders.
  - Camera uses an Orthographic (isometric) projection rather than dramatic perspective distortion.
- **Timeline & Flight Path Tracking:**
  - Minimalist glowing line tracing the movement of an aircraft, vehicle, or suspect.
  - Monospaced timestamp and GPS coordinate counter ticking smoothly in the lower-right corner.

---

## 3. Typography & Swiss Design

- **Primary Font:** DIN 1451, Helvetica Neue (Light & Medium), Univers, or Roboto Mono.
- **Layout:**
  - Monospaced forensic timestamps (`03:14:22 UTC`).
  - Strict grid alignment inspired by classic Swiss international typographic style.
  - Case file labels and exhibit tags (`EXHIBIT A`, `LOG #491`).

---

## 5. Sound Design & Ambient Drone Architecture

- **Voiceover:** Calm, Swedish/Nordic-inflected or low-mid broadcast baritone, unhurried and measured. Mastered with a flat, natural response.
- **Ambient Soundscapes:**
  - Deep analog synthesizer sub-drones that shift notes only once every 30 to 60 seconds.
  - Distant radio frequency static, tape hiss, and subtle Geiger counter clicks.
  - Subdued, heavy grandfather clock ticks counting down forensic timelines.\n
