# Kurzgesagt Style Editing (`kurzgesagt-style-editing`)

A production framework for coding agents and animators to create **world-class 2D vector animated science explainers** in the style of **Kurzgesagt – In a Nutshell**. Replicates their iconic design system: clean geometric vector art, cosmic scale transitions, existential humor, and majestic orchestral-electronic scoring.

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic DNA & Storytelling Engine

- **Tone & Persona:** Intellectually rigorous, existential, playful, awe-inspiring, optimistic nihilism.
- **Visual Stance:** Clean, flat 2D vector universe where complex scientific realities (microbiology, quantum physics, black holes) are distilled into charming, intuitive visual metaphors.
- **The Signature Motif:** The Kurzgesagt Duck—an expressive, innocent character used as a visual guinea pig to demonstrate extreme cosmic or biological scenarios.

---

## 3. Vector Design Language & Geometry

- **Geometric Purity:** Every character, planet, and cell is built from pure geometric primitives (circles, rounded rectangles, smooth bezier curves).
- **No Gradients on Characters:** Characters and objects use flat, bold colors. Gradients are reserved exclusively for atmospheres, glowing nebulas, and laser beams.
- **Thick Outlines:** Important characters feature clean, dark keylines (`4px` to `8px`) with rounded joins.
