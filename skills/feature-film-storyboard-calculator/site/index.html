Good to know

Assumptions, plainly.

Where do 34,000 words and 80 scenes come from?

They're planning estimates for a 170-minute feature at roughly one page per minute. Real scripts vary with dialogue density, pacing and scene length, so adjust the sliders to match yours.

Is Pro always cheaper?

No. The planner compares total spend, subscription plus top-ups, for both plans and recommends whichever is lower for your inputs.

Does formatting cost more if I rewrite sections?

Yes. The estimate assumes one pass through the screenplay. Repeated text requests or failed calls use extra credits.

Are my inputs saved?

No. The planner runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.
