How is this calculated?
  • Each storyboard image costs 15 credits.
  • Storyboard credits = scenes × attempts per scene × 15.
  • Formatting credits = rounded-up word count ÷ 250.
  • Base includes 500 credits; Pro includes 1,000 credits.
  • Each top-up adds 500 credits for ₹1,500. Top-ups are used after plan credits and do not expire.
  • Costs compare one subscription month plus enough top-ups to cover the project.
  • Plan limits on shots per scene are separate from this simplified 1–3 image-attempt estimate.
