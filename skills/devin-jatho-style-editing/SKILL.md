# Devin Jatho Style Editing (`devin-jatho-style-editing`)

A production framework for coding agents and editors to craft **clean, authoritative, creator-led social content** in the style of **Devin Jatho**. Strips away tacky visual clutter in favor of high-credibility framing, crisp direct-to-camera hooks, tasteful kinetic subtitles, and floating social/metrics proof cards.

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic DNA & Brand Tone

- **Tone & Persona:** Direct, authentic, strategic, commercially sharp, authoritative.
- **Visual Stance:** Clean, trustworthy, no-nonsense. Avoids childish cartoon stickers; relies on real-world proof (analytics screenshots, tweets, Stripe dashboards, customer messages).
- **Core Strategy:** Hook the viewer with an undeniable business reality in the first 2 seconds, deliver the tactical insight with high clarity, and close with a clear CTA.

---

## 2. Timeline Architecture & Pacing Rules

- **Target Pacing:** 1.5 to 2.5 seconds per visual transition. Paced to sound energetic and conversational without feeling chaotic.
- **Cut Structure:**
  - Seamless jump-cuts removing dead air.
  - Alternating scale punch-in (100% $
ightarrow$ 110%) synchronized to natural sentence breaks.
  - Proof cards held for 2.0 to 3.0 seconds to allow viewers to read the metric.

---

## 3. Subtitles & Proof Card Design

- **Subtitles:**
  - Centered in the middle-lower third (safe for Instagram/TikTok UI).
  - Modern geometric sans-serif (Inter, Satoshi, Helvetica Now).
  - White text with subtle black outline or soft background pill; single keyword highlighted in amber orange (`#FF6B00`) or golden yellow (`#FFB800`).
- **Floating Social & Metric Cards:**
  - White or dark-mode rounded rectangle card (`Border Radius: 24px`).
  - Realistic multi-layered iOS shadow (`Y: 16px`, `Blur: 40px`, `Opacity: 12%`).
  - Card slides up from bottom with subtle spring physics.

---

## 4. Color Grading & Studio Look

- **Color Tone:** Clean, neutral, high-clarity commercial grade.
- **Skin Tones:** Natural, warm, well-lit; no hyper-saturated artificial bronze.
- **Lighting:** Soft key light (large softbox at 45°) with subtle background practical light creating depth.

---

## 5. Sound Design & Audio Architecture

- **Voice Quality:** Studio-grade broadcast vocal (Rode PodMic or Shure SM7B). Crisp presence boost at `8kHz` for mobile speaker clarity.
- **Transitions:** Subtle air whooshes and paper snaps on card reveals.
- **Background Music:** Low-tempo chill-hop or ambient lo-fi, mastered very quietly (`-24dB`) so the spoken message remains front and center.\n
