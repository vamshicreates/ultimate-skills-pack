# YouTube Upload Policies & Compliance Checklist (2026 Reference)

Before and during every YouTube upload, the agent MUST verify and enforce compliance with all current YouTube Studio upload policies. Failing to select the proper disclosures or violating metadata policies can cause videos to be suppressed by the algorithm, demonetized, or removed.

---

## 1. Altered or Synthetic Content Policy (AI-Generated Content Disclosure) — MANDATORY

YouTube requires creators to disclose whenever a video contains realistic **Altered or Synthetic Content** created or modified using generative AI tools (such as Nano Banana / `generate_image`, Google Flow, Imagen, Veo, Sora, Runway, Suno, Udio, or voice cloning).

### When You MUST Select **"Yes"** under *"Altered content"*:
1. **Realistic AI-Generated Visuals or Scenes**: The video depicts realistic-looking scenes, temples, landscapes, characters, or events generated by AI that did not actually occur in the physical world (e.g., cinematic 35mm film-style AI slideshows, realistic devotional scenes, photorealistic storyboards).
2. **Synthetic / Cloned Voice or Singing**: The audio track uses an AI-generated voice or synthetic vocal performance that sounds like a real human singer or speaker.
3. **Altered Real Footage**: Real footage or audio was digitally altered by AI to change what someone said or did, or to alter a real place/event.

### How to Set It in YouTube Studio:
- In **Step 1: Details** -> scroll down and click **`"Show more"`**.
- Locate the **"Altered content"** section (*"Do any of the following describe your content?"*).
- Select the **`"Yes"`** radio button (`tp-yt-paper-radio-button[name="VIDEO_HAS_ALTERED_CONTENT_YES"]` or label `"Yes"` under Altered content).
- Also include a brief, transparent production note at the bottom of the video description (e.g., *"Visuals crafted with AI-assisted cinematic illustration & edited for this official lyric video."*).

## 2. Audience & COPPA Policy ("Made for Kids") — MANDATORY

Every upload on YouTube requires an explicit COPPA audience declaration before the **"Next"** or **"Publish"** button will activate.

### Which Option to Select:
- **Default for Music, Lyric Videos, Devotional Songs, Indie/Pop, Tutorials, Vlogs**:
  - Select **`"No, it's not made for kids"`** (`VIDEO_MADE_FOR_KIDS_NOT_MFK`).
  - **CRITICAL WARNING**: If you accidentally select *"Yes, it's made for kids"*, YouTube **permanently disables comments**, **disables pinned comments**, **disables end screens**, **disables info cards**, **disables miniplayer playback**, and **disables subscriber notification bells** on the video. This destroys engagement and view velocity!
- **Only Select `"Yes, it's made for kids"` If**:
  - The video is specifically created primarily for young children (e.g., nursery rhymes, ABC/counting animations for toddlers).
- **Age Restriction (Advanced)**:
  - Keep set to **`"No, don't restrict my video to viewers over 18 only"`** unless the lyrics/visuals contain explicit adult themes.

---

## 3. Metadata, Spam & Deceptive Practices Policy

YouTube's LLM-based content classifiers strictly penalize metadata spam:

1. **No Tag Stuffing in the Description**:
   - **NEVER** paste a block of comma-separated tags or a wall of unrelated keywords at the bottom of the description. This violates YouTube's **Misleading Metadata & Spam Policy** and can result in community strikes or algorithmic suppression.
   - Instead, weave primary and secondary keywords naturally into human-readable sentences in the *"About the Song"* section, and include the **Full Formatted Lyrics** (which legitimately contains all lyric phrases for search indexing).
2. **Hashtag Limit Policy (<= 15 Hashtags Strictly, 3–5 Optimal)**:
   - YouTube's official policy states that if a video has **more than 15 hashtags** in its title/description, **YouTube ignores ALL hashtags** on that video and may flag it as spam.
   - **Rule**: Use **3 to 5 hyper-relevant hashtags** at the very bottom of the description. The first 3 hashtags automatically appear as clickable pills directly above the video title on the watch page.
3. **Backend Tags Limit (`<= 500` Characters Total)**:
   - The YouTube Studio `Tags` input box (under `Details` -> `Show more`) accepts a maximum of **500 characters** total (including commas and spaces between multi-word tags).
   - Exceeding 500 characters triggers a red validation error in YouTube Studio and blocks the **Publish** button until excess tags are deleted.
4. **Honest Titles & Thumbnails**:
   - Titles (`<= 100` characters) and custom thumbnails must accurately reflect the song/video content without deceptive clickbait unrelated to the video.

## 4. Copyright, Content ID & Audio Policy (`Checks` Step)

1. During **Step 3: Checks**, YouTube Studio scans the uploaded video and audio stream for Copyright (Content ID) matches and Ad Suitability.
2. **Wait for Checks to Complete**:
   - Before clicking Publish (or at least while verifying the upload), inspect the **Checks** tab.
   - Verify that **Copyright** displays **`"No issues found"`** (green checkmark).
   - If a Content ID claim appears on third-party audio, alert the user in chat with the exact claim details (e.g., *"Visibility unaffected / Revenue shared"* vs. *"Blocked in some territories"*) before making the video Public.

---

## 5. Paid Promotion & Sponsorship Policy

- In **Step 1: Details** -> **`"Show more"`** -> **"Paid promotion"**:
  - Leave **`"My video contains paid promotion like a product placement, sponsorship, or endorsement"`** **UNCHECKED** unless the user explicitly states the video is a paid sponsorship.

---

## 6. Shorts Remixing, Embedding & Comments Policy

To maximize organic reach and algorithmic view velocity:
1. **Embedding & Subscriptions Feed**:
   - Ensure **`"Allow embedding"`** is **CHECKED**.
   - Ensure **`"Publish to subscriptions feed and notify subscribers"`** is **CHECKED** so every subscriber gets a push/home feed notification immediately upon publish.
2. **Shorts Remixing**:
   - Select **`"Allow video and audio remixing"`** so viewers and creators can use the song's audio or clips in YouTube Shorts, which links directly back to the original long-form video.
3. **Comments & Ratings**:
   - Set Comments to **`"On"`** (with `"Basic"` moderation) and Sort By to **`"Top"`** so the creator's **Pinned Engagement Comment** remains locked at the top of the comment section.
   - Ensure **`"Show how many viewers like this video"`** is **CHECKED**.
