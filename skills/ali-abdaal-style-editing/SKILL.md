# Ali Abdaal Style Editing (`ali-abdaal-style-editing`)

A production framework for coding agents and editors to assemble **clean, engaging, and friendly educational productivity videos** in the style of **Ali Abdaal**. Focuses on pedagogical clarity, high-key studio aesthetics, structured chapter navigation, and clean, joyful motion graphics.

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic DNA & Editorial Philosophy

- **Tone & Persona:** Friendly, intellectual, humble, structured, enthusiastic, clear.
- **Visual Stance:** *"Make learning delightful and effortless."* Complex ideas are organized into clean lists, digital book quotes, and step-by-step frameworks.
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, sunlit, organized modern studio with warm fairy lights or ambient practical lamps.

---

## 2. Timeline Architecture & Pacing Rules

- **Structure:** Clear 3-part pedagogical formula:
  1. *The Hook & Promise:* What you will learn and why it matters to your happiness/output.
  2. *Numbered Framework / Chapters:* Clear chapter markers with on-screen visual progress badges.
  3. *Actionable Summary & Book Recommendations:* Concrete next steps.
- **Cut Frequency:**
  - A-roll cut every 4.0 to 6.0 seconds using smooth scale punch-ins (`100%` to `112%`).
  - Chapter cards and UI overlays appear every 10 to 15 seconds to re-anchor viewer attention.

---

## 3. UI Cards, Book Overlays & Graphics

- **Kindle / Book Highlight Effect:**
  - Book cover floats in on the left side with a soft drop shadow (`Blur: 35px`, `Opacity: 15%`).
  - Excerpt text appears in clean Georgia serif font.
  - Neon pastel yellow (`#FFF9A6`) highlight line sweeps across the key quote.
- **Numbered Chapter Badges:**
  - Rounded pill badges: Circle with number `01`, `02`, `03` followed by chapter title.
  - Subtle spring bounce on arrival.
- **iPad Screen Ingestion:**
  - iPad screen framed inside a sleek Apple iPad mockup with rounded corners (`Radius: 24px`).
  - Animated Apple Pencil stroke drawing diagrams or mind-maps.

---

## 4. Typography & Title Design

- **Primary Font:** Circular Std, Inter, SF Pro Display, or Poppins (Medium & Bold weights).
- **Quote Font:** Georgia or Minion Pro (Italic).
- **Text Styling:**
  - Clean black (`#18181B`) on white cards, or pure white on dark backgrounds.
  - Never use aggressive 3D extrusions or heavy dropshadows; stick to soft diffuse blurs.

---

| Element | Hex Code | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Pure Studio White | `#FFFFFF` | Background cards, crisp UI panels |
| Soft Slate | `#18181B` | Primary typography |
| Productivity Yellow | `#FEEAA1` | Book highlights, focus accents |
| Pastel Mint | `#A7F3D0` | Action items, positive outcomes |
| Soft Lilac | `#DDD6FE` | Concepts, chapter pill badges |

---

## 6. Sound Design & Audio Architecture

- **Voiceover:** Natural, warm, conversational. Recorded via a clean shotgun or lavalier (e.g. Sennheiser MKH 416 or Rode Wireless Pro). Mastered to `-15 LUFS`.
- **Micro-UI Foley:**
  - Bubble pop / wooden block pop: On every bullet point, badge, or card entry.
  - Gentle paper whoosh: On chapter wipes.
  - Mouse click / tap: When navigating websites or Notion setups.
- **Music Bed:** Chill lo-fi study beats, acoustic guitar fingerpicking, or warm electric piano tracks. Music volume is polite and steady at `-20dB`.\n
