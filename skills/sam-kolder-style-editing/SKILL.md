# Sam Kolder Style Editing (`sam-kolder-style-editing`)

A production framework for coding agents and editors to craft **masterclass cinematic travel films and seamless motion-flow sequences** in the style of **Sam Kolder (@kold)**. Defines the standard for fluid visual storytelling: match-cuts, speed ramps, sky replacements, and emotional acoustic-cinematic soundscapes.

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic DNA & Visual Flow

- **Tone & Persona:** Adventurous, breathtaking, contemplative, cinematic, wanderlust-inducing.
- **The Core Rule:** *"Perpetual Motion."* Every shot moves in a direction that flows naturally into the next shot. The viewer never feels an abrupt cut; they feel swept along an uninterrupted visual journey.
- **Key Techniques:** The "Kolder Cut" (motion match cuts), directional speed ramping, whip-pan transitions, mask reveals behind foreground objects, and day-to-night sky replacements.

---

## 2. Speed Ramping & Optical Flow Mechanics

- **Shooting Specification:** Captured at high frame rates (`4K 60fps` or `120fps`) with shutter speed set strictly to the `180° rule` (`1/120s` or `1/240s`).
- **Velocity Curve Shaping:**
  - *Phase 1:* Normal/slow-motion movement (`100%` or `40%` speed).
  - *Phase 2 (The Ramp):* Sudden acceleration up to `800%` speed over `8–12 frames` with Optical Flow motion blur.
  - *Phase 3 (The Cut):* Cut happens at the absolute peak of motion blur.
  - *Phase 4 (The Arrival):* Incoming shot begins at `800%` speed and decelerates back down to smooth slow-motion.

---

## 3. Seamless Transition Catalog

1. **The Match Cut (Motion Vector):** Jumping off a cliff in Bali cuts seamlessly into diving into an Icelandic glacial lake, matching the subject's posture and downward velocity.
2. **Foreground Mask Wipe:** As a tree, pillar, or person crosses directly in front of the lens, their edge is rotoscoped as a moving matte revealing an entirely new continent.
3. **Sky Replacement / Luma Fade:** Bright daylight sky dissolves through a luma key into a star-filled Milky Way time-lapse while the terrain remains anchored.

---

## 4. Color Grading: The Definitive Teal & Orange

- **Color Split:**
  - Shadows & Mid-Darks: Pushed toward deep cyan/teal (`Hue: 195°`).
  - Highlights & Skin Tones: Protected and pushed toward warm golden bronze (`Hue: 35°`).
- **Contrast:** Film-like roll-off; blacks lifted slightly to `IRE 5`, highlights rolled off smoothly at `IRE 95`.

---

## 5. Sound Design & Atmospheric Immersion

- **The Soundscape Foundation:**
  - Deep natural ambient stems: Ocean roar, wind howling through peaks, city street hum.
  - Spatial Foley: Splashes, crunching gravel, cloth flutters, sharp inhalations before dives.
- **Musical Scoring:** Cinematic indie-folk, sweeping orchestral synths, or atmospheric electronic builds with heavy bass drops synchronized to speed-ramp landings.\n
