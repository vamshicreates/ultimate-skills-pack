## 1 · AASTHI — full premium product film (the house style)

Create a complete 30-second, 16:9 premium fintech motion-design film for AASTHI, a platform that lets NRIs invest in India's private markets from anywhere.

Style: CRED-style luxury product film. Seamless studio void, glossy product lighting, slow weightless camera, elegant lowercase serif typography, no people, no hard cuts. One continuous flowing film.

@Image2 = "How It Works" light step-card page on laptop.
@Image3 = AASTHI violet "A" logo. Preserve its exact shape, gradient and proportions. Do not invent another logo.
The laptop is the same silver MacBook shown in the references. Screen content must stay locked to the reference images: do not redesign, rearrange or re-letter the UI.

CREATIVE APPROACH
The story:
You moved abroad → your investments don't have to → AASTHI brings India's private markets to your laptop → apply, verify, get access → discover, review, choose → track everything in one place → built on trust → AASTHI.
The laptop is the hero object. UI elements peel off the screen, float in 3D depth and return. Typography floats in its own layer in front of or beside the laptop, never on the screen.

## 2 · Lumo — concept → kinetic SaaS film (only a logo was given)

Style: Contemporary kinetic typography film. Bold geometric sans, graphic colour fields, shapes that travel with purpose, beat-synced reveals. Confident and playful, never chaotic. No people, no footage, no real platform logos.

REFERENCES

CREATIVE APPROACH

VISUAL SYSTEM

A lime glowing tube draws across the lower third from left to right with a bright head, threading behind the text. 00:13–00:14 | WHIP

Dark field. Left-aligned cream: "3 hours of captions." Below, after 0.8 s, lime: "done in 3 minutes." with a lime-to-emerald highlight block wiping behind "3 minutes". The rosette rolls in and settles as a bullet beside the first line.

## 5 · Faceless explainer — "How end-to-end encryption works", 40 s

Create a complete 40-second, 16:9 faceless explainer film titled "How end-to-end encryption works", for a general audience.

Style: Editorial calm. Warm paper background, ink-drawn diagrams that build step by step, one idea per frame, a slow confident pace. No people, no brand logos, no numbers.

CREATIVE APPROACH
The story: you send a message → who can read it? → a lock only your friend can open → keys: one public, one private → the message travels locked → only their key opens it → even the app can't read it.
Hero device: two phone outlines (left = you, right = your friend) that stay in the same place the whole film; everything happens on the line between them.

VISUAL SYSTEM

Typography: Fraunces 400 for headlines, 84 px, left-aligned top-left at x = 140; JetBrains Mono 400, 30 px, for labels.
Drawing: 3 px ink strokes, round caps; everything draws on (line-draw) before it moves.

MOVEMENT
Editorial calm: draws 1.0–1.4 s power2.inOut; moves 1.2 s power3.out; crossfades between steps; one mask-rise per headline; no whips.

EXACT 40-SECOND CHOREOGRAPHY
00:00–00:05 | YOU SEND A MESSAGE

00:18–00:25 | TWO KEYS

00:25–00:32 | THE JOURNEY

00:32–00:37 | ONLY THEIR KEY

00:37–00:40 | EVEN THE APP
Everything except the two phones fades. Headline centred: "not even the app can read it." Finish by 00:38.5; hold to 00:40.

QUALITY AND CONSTRAINTS
