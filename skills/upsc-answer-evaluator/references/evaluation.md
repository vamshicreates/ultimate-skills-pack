# Evaluation decisions

## Question demand

Identify the directive, topic, scope, qualifiers, and required parts. For 'examine', expect explanation and assessment of the stated relationship, supported by relevant evidence; listing facts is insufficient. Build a short expected-coverage outline independently of the submitted answer, but do not require one coaching institute's preferred wording.

## Qualitative rubric

Assess six dimensions, grounding judgments in the answer:

| Dimension | Look for |
| --- | --- |
| Demand coverage | Direct response to all parts; balance appropriate to the question |
| Accuracy | Correct provisions, chronology, examples, definitions and attributions |
| Reasoning | Why/how explanations; links between claim, evidence and implication |
| Evidence | Relevant cases, data and examples explained rather than merely named |
| Structure | Purposeful introduction, connected body and reasoned conclusion |
| Expression | Readable prose, precise language and economical use of space |

Do not penalize handwriting style except where legibility impedes understanding. Do not reward decoration or article counts as substitutes for reasoning. When word limit is unknown, give editing advice without a compliance judgment. Numerical scores need a known maximum, explicit rubric, user authorization and calibration caveat; never translate an arbitrary percentage into a purported UPSC prediction.

## Evidence record

For every consequential checked claim record: page/excerpt, status, rationale, source IDs, and precise source locator. A judgment's party submissions are not the holding. A commission's recommendation is not existing law. The authenticity of an event does not establish partisan intent. A later opinion may qualify an earlier decision without making every historical observation false.

Do not infer a date from an attachment filename as a confirmed exam date. State the actual evaluation cutoff. Do not penalize omission of developments after a confirmed historical cutoff. For unresolved transcription, store alternatives and avoid deductions depending on the disputed reading.

## Concision and transfer

Prioritize errors or omissions that change the argument. Give three actions that the student can apply on the next attempt. The model outline should improve the answer within the known word budget, or be labelled an outline when no budget is supplied. For this prototype, do not add predictions or long subject notes to the learner report.
