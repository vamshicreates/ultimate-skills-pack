# Editorial rules

## Source fidelity

- Base the copy on the supplied article, not the headline alone.
- Preserve attribution such as “police said,” “according to the report,” or the natural Telugu equivalent.
- Preserve uncertainty with wording such as “expected,” “reportedly,” “may,” or “alleged.”
- Do not add unsourced claims, quotations, statistics, dates, motives, or outcomes.
- If the article is inaccessible or too incomplete to verify, request another link or the article text.

## Search-friendly writing

- Put the primary named entity and the concrete event in natural language near the beginning.
- Use recognizable names, places, organizations, titles, and topic terms that a reader might search for.
- Write for clarity and relevance. Do not promise ranking or exploit unrelated trending terms.
- Use zero to two directly relevant hashtags. Avoid hashtag stuffing and unrelated trends.
- Prefer specific nouns and active verbs over generic phrases such as “big update” or “shocking news.”
- Keep the article URL in the news content when it fits. Do not put app-store links in the news content; those belong in the mandatory reply.

## Single post

- Lead with the verified development.
- Add one useful detail or piece of context.
- Keep the entire post within the live X composer limit, including the article URL and hashtags.
- Use a direct, professional news tone. Avoid sensational or vague clickbait.

## Thread

- Use only as many posts as the story needs, normally three to seven.
- Make the first post a complete summary and a clear reason to continue.
- Give each following post one job: key fact, context, quote, impact, or what happens next.
- Number posts consistently, such as `1/5`, after the final thread length is known.
- Keep every post within the live X composer limit.
- Place the article URL in the final news post unless the story benefits from having it in the opening post.
- Attach the mandatory app reply after the final numbered post. Do not number the app reply as part of the thread.

## Language

- Write English for English readers and idiomatic Telugu for Telugu readers; do not produce rigid word-for-word translations.
- Preserve official names and commonly recognized spellings.
- Keep the requested language consistent throughout the post or thread.

## Final review

Confirm that:

- every factual claim is supported by the article;
- the lead identifies the actual news;
- names, numbers, dates, and links are accurate;
- no post is duplicated or substantially repeated;
- the mandatory app reply exactly matches the text in `SKILL.md`.
