# Vamsi Creates carousel design system

This reference captures the approved 10-slide solo-builder carousel. Apply the visual system to other topics without forcing its exact wording or number of slides.

## Canvas and colors

- Default canvas: Instagram portrait, **4:5**, preferably **1080 × 1350 px**. The reference Canva design uses 1036 × 1296 px; scale its measurements proportionally.
- Main background: white `#FFFFFF`.
- Accent blue: `#3B82F6` for the narrow top rule and small accents.
- Emphasis blue: `#2563EB` for key phrases, handles, section headings, and CTA keywords.
- Main ink: `#0F172A`.
- Muted text: `#475569`, only where a secondary tier is needed.
- Light divider/border: `#D9E2EE`.
- CTA background: dark navy `#102A58`.
- Optional pale blue surface: `#EFF6FF`.

Keep white and navy areas dominant. Use blue to show hierarchy, not to color every line.

## Typography

Use **Poppins only**. Use bold or semibold for hooks, names, numbers, and large section prompts; regular for explanation. Maintain generous line spacing and avoid tiny type.

Reference sizes on a 1036 × 1296 px page:

| Role | Reference size | Treatment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Cover opener | 42 px | Bold, dark ink |
| Cover emphasized line | 49 px | Bold, emphasis blue |
| Content title | 50 px | Bold, dark ink; reduce slightly only for long names |
| Subtitle or handle | 24 px | Bold, emphasis blue |
| Small label | 15 px | Bold, uppercase, emphasis blue |
| Key value or product line | 29 px | Bold, dark ink |
| Takeaway heading | 40 px | Bold, emphasis blue |
| Explanation | 30 px | Regular, dark ink |
| CTA keyword | 76 px | Bold, emphasis blue on white pill |

These are starting values. Keep the same relative hierarchy if Canva renders a font differently or the user chooses another canvas size. Add a slide before reducing essential copy below comfortable mobile reading size.

## Page patterns

### Cover

- Thin blue rule at the top.
- Direct statement in the upper third, preferably two or three short lines with one blue emphasis line.
- One relevant, uncluttered image in the lower half if it helps; keep the headline off busy imagery.
- Leave enough empty space around the hook to read it instantly.

### Content slide

- Thin blue top rule; white background.
- Large left-aligned title at roughly 7% from the left and 5% from the top. Optional blue subtitle directly underneath.
- Central visual card or evidence block begins near 19% from the top, around 80% of page width and 35% of page height. For real profiles or products, use a clean screenshot in a rounded light border. Crop consistently without losing the identifying part.
- Light divider around 57% from the top. Then one small blue label and one bold value line, if the content needs that structure.
- Large blue takeaway heading around 69%, with a brief explanation below. Replace labels such as “PRODUCTS” and “WHY FOLLOW?” with topic-appropriate labels.
- Keep generous whitespace at the bottom. A slide should communicate one point at a glance.

### Closing CTA

- Dark navy background with a thin blue top rule.
- Short white action verb or instruction.
- White rounded keyword block with large blue Poppins text when a comment or DM keyword is part of the user's CTA.
- One concise white follow-on line stating what the audience receives.

## Readability and editability checks

- Every text element uses Poppins; check the cover, a typical content slide, and the CTA in Canva's font control.
- No text is clipped or crowded by an image, border, or divider. All repeated cards, dividers, and text blocks line up across similar slides.
- Screenshots remain identifiable at mobile size; avoid enlarging low-resolution captures beyond what stays legible.
- All text and images remain separately editable in Canva. Return the design's edit link, not an exported image alone.
