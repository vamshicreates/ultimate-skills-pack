# Natt Jongsala Style Editing (`natt-jongsala-style-editing`)

A production framework for coding agents and editors to produce **high-end spatial composite short-form videos** in the style of **Natt Jongsala (@nattjongsala)**. Reconstructs flat talking-head footage into rich, multi-layered 3D spatial environments with rotoscoped foregrounds, glowing architectural grids, and synchronized kinetic typography.

---

## 1. Core Aesthetic DNA & Spatial Philosophy

- **Tone & Persona:** Cinematic, hyper-modern, hypnotic, architecturally precise.
- **The Core Technique:** The speaker is separated from their physical room and placed into a bespoke virtual stage with foreground, midground, and background depth planes.
- **Visual Stance:** Every scene feels like an interactive 3D digital world where information surrounds the presenter.

---

## 2. Multi-Plane Layer Stack (The 5-Layer System)

1. **Layer 1 (Foreground Overlays):** Floating translucent glass HUD panels, dust particles, and cinematic lens flares.
2. **Layer 2 (Rotoscoped Subject):** The presenter rotoscoped frame-by-frame (Runway Gen-2, Magic Mask in DaVinci, or Roto Brush 3 in After Effects), with refined hair edges and light-wrap.
3. **Layer 3 (Midground Graphic Anchors):** Floating 3D text cards, financial charts, or concept illustrations placed *directly behind* the speaker's shoulders.
4. **Layer 4 (Background Environment):** Dark architectural grid, infinite 3D perspective floor, or moody concrete studio wall.
5. **Layer 5 (Ambient Backlight / Rim Glow):** Large soft radial glow or rim light casting colored light onto the back of the presenter's silhouette.

---

## 3. DaVinci Resolve Fusion Node Tree Architecture

```
[MediaIn (A-Roll)] ---> [Magic Mask (Roto)] ---> [Light Wrap] ------------------------                                                       ^                               [Background Grid] ---> [3D Camera Tracker] ---> [Text3D / Shape3D] ---> [Merge3D] ---> [Renderer3D] ---> [Merge] ---> [MediaOut]
```

- **Light Wrap Pass:** Blends background illumination around the subject's edges to eliminate the harsh "green screen cutout" appearance.
- **Depth Blur:** Background elements are treated with a realistic Depth of Field blur (focal point locked to the speaker's eyes).

---

## 4. Typography & Title Integration

- **Primary Font:** Helvetica Neue Extended, Formula Condensed, or Editorial New.
- **Positioning:** Locked in 3D camera space behind the presenter. As the presenter moves, they physically occlude the text, creating powerful three-dimensional immersion.
- **Tracking:** Kinetic letter-spacing that tightens as the point is emphasized.

---

## 5. Color Grading & Studio Palette

- **Grade:** Cinematic desaturated grade with rich skin tones.
- **Palette:** Muted charcoal background (`#121316`), deep forest green or slate blue shadows, warm radiant skin highlights (`#FFDFC4`), and glowing cyan UI elements (`#00F2FE`).

---

## 6. Sound Design & Spatial Audio

- **Spatial Whooshes:** Stereo-panned whooshes that travel from left to right as graphic elements fly past the camera.
- **Impacts:** Heavy low-end sub-booms when transitioning between major conceptual environments.\n
