# Sri Priyatham Style DNA & Analysis

This reference document details the signature stylistic characteristics extracted from the 214-artwork archive of Sri Priyatham.

---

## 1. Digital Illustration & Concept Art
- **Visual Identity**: Rich painterly textures, atmospheric lighting, rim light highlights, and deliberate color harmony.
- **Palette**: Often uses complementary contrasts (teal/orange, magenta/cyan, warm amber key light against cool navy/slate shadows).
- **Brushwork**: Visible textured brushwork that mimics traditional oil or gouache in Procreate/Photoshop; sharp edge control with soft gradient ambient occlusion.
- **Composition**: Cinematic framing, focal isolation with depth-of-field blur in backgrounds, high narrative emotion.

---

## 2. Pencil Sketches & Anatomical Studies
- **Visual Identity**: Traditional graphite/lead pencil on textured paper/toned sketchbook background.
- **Linework**: Dynamic construction lines, under-drawing scaffolding left visible, loose energetic gesture lines paired with tight rendered focal points.
- **Shading**: Directional cross-hatching, pressure-sensitive graphite value scale (from 2H light guides to 6B deep darks).
- **Subject Focus**: Head planes, facial anatomy (Asaro head structure), planar lighting, hand gesture breakdowns, organic muscular tension.

---

## 3. Caricatures & Expressive Portraits
- **Visual Identity**: Exaggerated likeness while maintaining immediate recognition and warmth (never grotesque, always honoring the subject).
- **Facial Architecture**: Accentuates defining features—jawlines, nose bridges, expressive brow furrows, unique eye spacing, dental nuances, ear silhouettes.
- **Expression**: Dynamic micro-expressions (quizzical smirk, hearty laugh, intense cinematic stare, contemplative gaze).
- **Technique**: Bold contour lines framing stylized color gradients and dimensional planar shading.

---

## 4. Inktober & Fine Ink Work
- **Visual Identity**: Crisp pen & ink, Micron/Rotring fineliner precision, dip pen nib variations, and rich India ink black levels.
- **Texture**: Meticulous cross-hatching, stippling, contour hatching, and selective ink wash / negative space contrasts.
- **Subject Focus**: Mythological figures, traditional Indian artifacts, flora/fauna, street scenes, and everyday objects drawn with sacred precision.
